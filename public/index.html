How to Play

  1. Insert a lesson card. Each card has 20 numbered spots on the disc and a clue for every spot.
  2. Watch the question light. The lamp beside the board shows which question you're on; the clue appears on the card strip.
  3. Answer. Type the spot's number on the keypad and press GO, or tap the numbered spot. In Map hunt mode, there are no numbers: tap the place itself.
  4. Three tries. First-try answers score the most, plus a time bonus for every second left on the clock and a streak bonus for runs of correct answers.
  5. Two players take turns, each with their own red score display. After a miss, the correct spot flashes so everybody learns it.
  6. HINT names the place (or circles the area in Map hunt) for half points.
